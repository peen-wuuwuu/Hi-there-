# Demon Horde w/ Mercenary Followers **Version 1.91**

##  MERCENARY FOLLOWERS
- Mercenaries now follow their hiring player, engage enemies, and regroup when idle.
- Improved health and regeneration.
- Hire for **500 gold** instead of 1,000.
- Mercenaries are available throughout each map.
- **Recommend having at least one or two with the team on harder maps.**

##  MAP ROTATION
- **Classic Maps:** Original Demon Horde maps with MercFollowers.
- **Hard Mode:** Reworked maps featuring additional objectives, miniboss encounters and increased max enemies.
- **DefendCastello:** Custom fortress defense scenario against an ongoing demon invasion.

##  GAMEPLAY BALANCING
- **Starting Gold:** 500 → 800
- **Starting Skill Points:** 5 → 10
- **Prestige:** Disabled

## CHANGES
- **Grad_Hard is fully fixed and playable again!**
- DefendCastello object colliders in some sections were fixed, and allies have been decreased throughout the level
- Exponential fog in Grad, Totenwald, and MtPeak maps have been increased
